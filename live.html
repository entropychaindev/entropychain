    Why nobody can pick the result
    
    
    The beacon is SHA-256d("entropy" + block hash). A block's hash only exists once its proof
      of work is solved, so not even the miner knows the beacon while they are still working on the block.
    
    
    To change the outcome, a miner would have to throw away a block they solved, and its 50 ENTRO
      reward with it, then solve a new one. That buys them another random roll, not the one they want.
      Lotteries can combine up to 16 blocks in a row, so steering one would mean discarding blocks over and over.
    
    
    Lottery winners on EntropyChain are chosen exactly the way the dice above are rolled:
      SHA-256(beacon + lottery ID) mod number of entrants.
