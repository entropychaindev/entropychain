Every roll decided by the chain.

Entropy Quest is a dungeon-raiding RPG on EntropyChain. You summon heroes, send them on expeditions, and bring back ENTRO and loot. Every outcome comes from the beacon of a block that hadn't been mined yet when you made your move, so nobody can predict or rig it, not even the game. This guide covers everything you need to play well.

01Getting started

1Get a walletCreate one at wallet.entropychain.io. Save the backup it gives you.
2Get ENTROGrab free testnet ENTRO from the faucet, or mine some with the desktop app.
3ConnectOn the game page, click Connect wallet. Your wallet opens in a small window: unlock it and approve.
4PlaySummon a hero (5 ENTRO), then raid the Goblin Warren (1 ENTRO).

Your keys stay in your wallet. The game never sees your private key. Every time the game wants to spend ENTRO, your wallet pops up and shows you exactly how much and what for. Nothing is sent until you approve it there.

If the wallet window doesn't open, your browser is probably blocking pop-ups for entropychain.io. Allow them and click again.

After you approve a move, the game shows its progress: sent → in a block → beacon revealed. The result is decided by the block after yours, so expect to wait one or two blocks.

02Heroes

Summoning costs 5 ENTRO. The next block's beacon decides your hero's class, five stats, a name, and a 3% chance of Starborn ✨, which adds +2 to every stat.

Each stat starts between 3 and 9 (most land around 6), then the class adds its bonus.

Your hero arrives in your wallet as an NFT. You can own as many heroes as you like and switch between them. Heroes never die permanently.

What's behind each door

Every run has four rooms, then the boss. Each of the four rooms is rolled:

⚔️ Monster 55% ⚠️ Trap 15% 🧰 Chest 15% ⛲ Shrine 15%
  • ⚔️ Monster: a fight. Win for gold (8–16% of the entry fee), XP and a 6% item chance.
  • ⚠️ Trap: roll d20 + DEX + level÷3 against 10 + 3×dungeon number. Make it and you gain XP; miss it and you take damage (less with more Defence).
  • 🧰 Chest: free gold (15–35% of the fee) and a 12% item chance.
  • ⛲ Shrine: heals up to 30% of your max HP. If you're already at full health, it's wasted.
  • ☠️ Boss: 2.5× a normal monster's HP and hits harder. Beat it for the big prize: 42–72% of the fee in gold and a 45% item chance.

Item chances above go up by your loot find.

04Combat

Fights are automatic, turn by turn. You strike first, then the enemy, until one side falls.

  • Your hit chance = Hit − enemy evasion, kept between 35% and 95%.
  • Your damage = Attack + 1d6 − 3 − enemy armour (at least 1). A crit doubles it.
  • Enemy hit chance = 80% − your Dodge, kept between 30% and 95%.
  • Enemy damage = its attack + 1d6 − 3 − your Defence (at least 1).
  • Class tricks apply on top: the Rogue's ambush, the Ranger's volley, the Mage's fireballs and the Warrior's blocks.
  • A fight lasts at most 30 rounds. If neither side falls, the monster slinks away and you get nothing for it.

Monsters get tougher in deeper dungeons. Each one is tuned so that a hero at the recommended level finds it about as hard as a new hero finds the Goblin Warren.

05Gold, death & XP

Gold

Gold you find is paid to your wallet in ENTRO right after the run, capped at 3× the entry fee.

Falling in battle

If your HP hits zero, your hero crawls out with half the gold found so far. They keep all the XP they earned. There is no permanent death.

07Why it's fair, and how to check

Each move is a transaction to the game. The result comes from the beacon of the next block: SHA256(SHA256("entropy" ‖ block hash)), mixed with your transaction's hash.

  • You can't pick a result: when you sign, the block that decides the outcome doesn't exist yet.
  • The game can't pick one either: the rules are fixed, open source, and anyone can recompute every run from the chain.
  • Miners can't cheaply steer it: a miner would have to throw away a valid block, and the reward with it, to try for a different beacon.

After any run, click 🔍 Verify this run in my browser. Your browser fetches the block from the chain, hashes it into the beacon, then replays the expedition with the game master's own code (compiled to WebAssembly). Two green checks mean the result is exactly what the rules say.

Every payout and item is also a normal transaction you can look up on the explorer.

08Tips

  • Stay on level. The dungeon cards say Fair fight, Risky or Deadly. Deadly means it.
  • Armor first. Running out of HP before the boss is the most common way to fall. Armor adds HP and Defence at once.
  • Charms pay off over time. Loot find means more drops, and better ones.
  • VIT matters for everyone. HP carries through all five rooms; there is no rest between them.
  • Build a party. Different classes suit different gear, and a Starborn summon is always possible.
  • Expect swings. On average a run returns less ENTRO than it costs; the real prizes are levels and item drops.

09FAQ

Is ENTRO real money?

No. Entropy Quest runs on EntropyChain's testnet. ENTRO here has no real-world value; it's free from the faucet.

How long does a run take?

Your transaction needs to land in a block, and then the next block reveals the beacon. That's usually a minute or two, depending on how fast blocks are being mined.

I paid the wrong amount. What happens?

The game refunds it in full and shows the reason under "Your runs". The same happens if you send a hero you don't own or pick a dungeon that doesn't exist. Only the tiny network fee is kept by the network.

Can I sell or give away a hero?

Yes. Heroes are NFTs, and their level and record go with them to the new owner.

My payout says "on its way".

The game sends payouts and items as soon as the run is decided; they show ✔ once the next block includes them. If blocks are slow, so is delivery.

Can the game change the rules?

Not quietly. Every run records the rules version it was played under (shown on the game page as "rules v1"), and the rules are public on GitHub. Any change that could alter a result has to come with a new version number.
